Marina Verde — a sun-soaked open-world crime game in the browser. Explore a procedural coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the marina police, and run five story jobs for Rosa and Marco — from your first stolen wheels to the grand getaway yacht. Built with three.js (vendored, offline), original art, synthesised WebAudio sound and music, a live minimap HUD, and a save system.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, just static files under `game/`.

## How to play

- **W A S D** — walk / drive · **Mouse** — look around · **Shift** — run
- **E** — enter / exit a car (stealing a car raises your wanted level)
- **Click** — shoot · **V** — punch · **P** — pause · **M** — mission brief
- **1–5 stars** — the marina police chase, ram and try to bust you. Stay clean and out of sight to lose the heat.
- **5 story missions** — Wheels Up, Sunset Courier, Heat Wave, The Drop, Grand Getaway. Finish them all and the city is yours.
