A sun-soaked coastal city of pastel towers, palm boulevards and one very bad idea. You are Nico Vale, a courier with no money and a fast-car problem. Rosa runs the crew and Marco drives getaway — twelve jobs for you: steal, deliver, tail, race, outrun the law and the helicopter, and make it to the yacht.

HOW TO PLAY

Drive — WASD to drive, Space to handbrake-drift. Press E near a car to get in; E on a moving car carjacks it (the law notices).
Jobs — follow the gold GPS line to the objective. 12 story jobs in 3 acts, plus a repeatable street race on the beachfront.
Heat — crimes raise wanted stars. Break line of sight and the MVPD starts SEARCHING; stay hidden 10 s to lose them.
Gear up — Verde Arms sells weapons, Rosa's Garage repairs & repaints, the Coral Clinic heals, the Safehouse saves.
Turf — finish jobs to raise your Turf Ledger rep and earn shop discounts, up to 15%.
