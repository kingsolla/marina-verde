# Controls - Marina Verde: Full Tide

## On foot / driving

- **W A S D** - walk / drive
- **Mouse** - look around
- **Shift** - run
- **Space** - handbrake / drift

## Interaction

- **E** - enter / exit a car, open a shop, carjack a moving traffic car (raises wanted level; +1 extra star if police are near)
- **Click** - shoot
- **V** - punch

## Weapons & radio

- **1-4** - select weapon (pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle)
- **Q** - cycle weapon
- **R** - cycle radio station (3 stations)

## System

- **P** - pause
- **M** - full map (districts, GPS route, Turf Ledger, 12-mission list)

## Wanted level

1-5 stars: the marina police chase, ram and try to bust you. At 4-5 stars a helicopter joins with a searchlight. Break line of sight and the MVPD switches to SEARCH - stay out of the search zone and unseen to lose the heat.
